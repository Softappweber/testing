soloDS Box ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we access and how we use it.

When you connect your Google Drive, we access:

• Basic Drive folder operations (create, list, rename)

3. How We Use Your Data

• Files are stored in YOUR Google Drive
• We do NOT store your files on our servers
• We do NOT sell or share your data

4. Data Storage

All files remain in your personal Google Drive. We only store:

• Your username
• Login token (for session management)
• Basic usage data (uploads, renames)

5. Data Deletion

You can disconnect access anytime through your Google Account settings. To delete your soloDS Box data, contact us at the email below.

6. Security

We use Google OAuth for authentication. Your files are protected by Google's security infrastructure.
